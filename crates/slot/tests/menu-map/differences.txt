# What the rg34xx's menus and the rgb20sx's differ in (menu_map.rs)

== In game: Atari Lynx
Settings > Screen > Bezel: rg34xx only
Settings > Screen > Stretch: rgb20sx only

== In game: Game Boy
Settings > Screen > Bezel: rg34xx "None", rgb20sx "AGS-102"
Settings > Screen > Scale: rg34xx "Integer", rgb20sx "SP Screen"
Settings > Screen > Stretch: rg34xx "Classic", rgb20sx "Off"
